Privacy policy

This policy explains what this QuizMaster installation stores and why. It is a template for an academic project; the operator of a real deployment must review it.

What we store

  • Account data: name, email, password hash (never the password itself), optional profile details and photo.
  • Learning data: quiz attempts, answers, scores and timestamps.
  • Security data: sign-in events, IP address of key actions (audit log) and hashed verification codes/reset tokens.
  • Messages: support messages and feedback you choose to send.

How we use it

Only to provide quizzes, grade them, show your progress, secure your account and improve the service. No advertising, no selling of data, no third-party analytics.

Cookies

A single essential session cookie (HTTP-only) keeps you signed in and protects forms against CSRF. Guest display preferences are kept in your browser’s local storage.

Leaderboards

You can hide yourself from leaderboards at any time in Settings.

Your rights

You can view and edit your profile at any time, see every attempt in your history, and permanently delete your account from Settings → Danger zone.

Contact

Questions? Use the contact form.
