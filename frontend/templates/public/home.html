FAQ

Questions, answered

Is QuizMaster free for students?

Yes. Create an account, verify your email and take any published quiz.

What happens if my internet disconnects during a quiz?

Your answers are kept on your device and synced automatically when the connection returns. The server timer keeps running, so reconnect as soon as you can.

When will I see my results?

That depends on the quiz: some release results immediately, others after the quiz closes or when the administrator publishes them. The quiz page always tells you which.

Can I retake a quiz?

If the administrator allows more than one attempt, yes — the quiz page shows how many attempts you have left.
