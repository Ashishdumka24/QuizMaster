About QuizMaster

Assessment built on quality principles

QuizMaster is an online quiz system developed for the BBAT104 Total Quality Management course (session 2026–27). Its core quality goal: ensure zero latency during submissions & auto-grading.

Who uses QuizMaster?

  • {{ icon('check') }} Students take quizzes, review explanations, track progress and download certificates.
  • {{ icon('check') }} Administrators author questions, run quizzes, release results, manage users and monitor quality.
  • {{ icon('check') }} Visitors can browse the public quiz catalogue and help centre before signing up.

Technology

Python 3 · Flask · SQLAlchemy · SQLite3 · Matplotlib (TQM charts) · vanilla JavaScript. No third-party trackers or analytics.
