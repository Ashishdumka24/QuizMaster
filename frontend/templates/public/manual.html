QuizMaster user manual

Version {{ app_version }}. A printable copy with screenshots is in docs/USER_MANUAL.md of the project repository.

1. Getting started

  1. Open Create account, enter your name, email and a strong password, and accept the terms.
  2. Check your email for a {{ config.OTP_LENGTH }}-digit code and enter it on the verification page. You can paste the whole code.
  3. You’re signed in and taken to your dashboard.

2. Taking a quiz

  1. Go to Explore and filter by subject, difficulty or availability.
  2. Open a quiz to read its rules: time limit, pass mark, attempts and negative marking.
  3. Press Start quiz. The timer starts on the server immediately.
  4. Choose answers; use Mark for review for questions you want to revisit. The navigator shows answered (filled), unanswered and marked (amber dot) questions.
  5. Press Submit quiz. A summary shows unanswered questions before you confirm.

Keyboard: 1–9 choose options, ←/→ move, M mark, X clear, S submit.

3. Results & history

When results are released you see your score, pass/fail, time taken and a per-question review (with correct answers and explanations if the quiz allows). History lists every attempt; Results groups released and pending results. Passed quizzes provide a printable certificate.

4. Profile & settings

  • Profile: update your name, institution, programme, bio and photo.
  • Security: change your password and sign out of other devices.
  • Settings: theme, colour palette, contrast, text size, reduced motion, leaderboard visibility and notifications. You can also delete your account.

5. Administrators

  1. Create categories, then add questions in the Question bank (or import CSV/JSON using the template).
  2. Create a quiz in Quizzes, configure timing, attempts, marking and result release, and add questions.
  3. Preview, then Publish. QuizMaster blocks publishing if a quiz has problems (no questions, invalid answers, etc.).
  4. Monitor Results, override short-answer grading if needed, and publish results for manually released quizzes.
  5. Use Announcements, Inbox, Audit logs, Backups and Settings to run the system.

6. TQM centre

Admins can log defects, view Pareto and fishbone charts, a control chart of submission latency, manage the FMEA (RPN) table, track PDCA cycles, view the SIPOC map and download a quality report.

7. Troubleshooting

  • “Security check failed”: the page was open too long. Refresh and try again.
  • “Too many requests”: wait a minute; limits protect accounts from abuse.
  • Offline during a quiz: keep the tab open; answers sync when you reconnect.
