Frequently asked questions

Account

I didn’t receive my verification code.

Check spam/promotions. On the verification page, press “Resend code” (available after a short cooldown). Each new code cancels older ones. Codes expire after {{ config.OTP_TTL_MINUTES }} minutes.

I forgot my password.

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page. We’ll email a single-use link valid for {{ config.RESET_TOKEN_TTL_MINUTES }} minutes. Resetting signs you out everywhere.

My account is locked.

After {{ config.LOGIN_MAX_FAILURES }} failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for {{ config.LOGIN_LOCK_MINUTES }} minutes to protect it. Wait, or reset your password.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Danger zone. Deletion is permanent and removes your attempts and profile photo.

Taking quizzes

What if I close the tab or lose connection?

Answers autosave and are also kept on your device until the server confirms them. Reopen the quiz from your dashboard to continue. The timer keeps running on the server.

What happens when time runs out?

Your saved answers are submitted automatically. Unanswered questions score zero.

What is negative marking?

On quizzes with negative marking, a wrong answer subtracts the stated marks. Leaving a question blank scores zero. Your total never goes below zero.

Why can’t I see the correct answers?

The administrator controls when results, correct answers and explanations are released — immediately, after the quiz closes, or manually.
