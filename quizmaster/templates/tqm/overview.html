TQM principles in this app

  • Customer focus — feedback ratings, bug reports, CSAT metric.
  • Kaizen — PDCA tracker and version log record each improvement.
  • Process-centric — SIPOC map of the quiz-taking process.
  • Fact-based decisions — Pareto, control chart and item analysis from live data.
  • Poka-yoke — server-enforced timer, idempotent submission, duplicate detection, validation.
